Questions

A few things people ask before getting started.

Where are my notes stored? +

All notes live on your own device. Nothing is uploaded or shared unless you choose to share it.

Does Slate need an internet connection? +

No. You can read and write notes entirely offline. Downloads are the only step that requires a connection.

Which platforms are supported? +

Linux, Windows, and macOS, with identical features on each.
